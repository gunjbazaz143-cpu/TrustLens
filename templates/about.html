About {{ site_name }}

{{ site_name }} is an AI-based information verification system built to fight phishing, payment scams, fake news, fraudulent job offers and misleading product labels.

Our mission

Scammers rely on urgency, fake scores and unverifiable claims. We built a tool that does the opposite: it gathers real evidence from the content you give it and shows you exactly why something looks trustworthy - or why it does not. If it cannot decide, it tells you it cannot decide.

What we analyse

  • Text and email - urgency language, malicious links, payment-to-reward patterns, scam-corpus similarity, grammar.
  • Images and screenshots - OCR with confidence, blur, compression, editing metadata, tampering (ELA), duplicate detection, embedded QR codes.
  • Websites - URL structure, WHOIS domain age, TLS, redirect chains, page content.
  • News - clickbait detection and live cross-checking against trusted feeds.
  • Jobs and internships - fee collection, free-mail recruiters, unrealistic salaries, missing company identifiers.
  • Claims - matched against a seeded knowledge base of verified facts.
  • Product ingredients - OCR label reading screened against a risk database.
  • QR codes - payload decoded and scored with the full text engine.

Honesty by design

We never fabricate a score. Every result page shows the evidence that produced the score, each with its point impact. When a scan lacks enough information - a two-word message, an unreachable site, an unreadable label - the verdict is Insufficient Evidence, not a guess.
