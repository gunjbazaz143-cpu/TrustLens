Claim checker

State a claim as a full sentence and we will compare it against a knowledge base of verified facts.

How it works
  • Your claim is compared against seeded evidence entries
  • A minimum similarity threshold must be met
  • Matched claims return "Likely True" or "Likely False" with the evidence shown
  • No match means Insufficient Evidence - never a guess
Tip

Phrase the claim the same way a fact-checker would. Short fragments rarely reach the threshold.
