What happens
  • The QR payload is decoded (with auto-rotation & preprocessing)
  • upi://pay QRs are fully parsed and the UPI ID validated with a strict regex — verdict "Valid" or "Suspicious" UPI QR with reasons
  • URL QRs get HTTPS, SSL certificate, domain-age and (when configured) Safe Browsing / VirusTotal checks
  • Text payloads run through the full text engine
Honesty rule

If no QR code is found in the image, we return "Insufficient Evidence" rather than guessing a score.
