What we screen
  • Edible foods: INS/E-numbers (627, 631, 551, 296, 102, 110, 129, 211...), trans fats, artificial dyes (Yellow 5, Red 40...), HFCS, potassium bromate, nitrites/nitrates, refined palm & palmolein oil
  • Cosmetics & skincare: parabens, phthalates, SLS/SLES, formaldehyde releasers (DMDM, Quaternium-15), synthetic fragrance & EU allergens (Hexyl Cinnamal, Citronellol, Linalool...), BHT/BHA, triclosan
  • Safe/GRAS lists: water/aqua, glycerin, aloe vera, argan oil, panthenol, hyaluronic acid, salicylic acid, citric acid, spices & natural starches
  • Fuzzy header detection: "INGREDIENTS", "GREDIENTS", "Ingred:", "Contains:", "Composition:" - cropped, partial or misspelled headers all work, with a comma-term fallback when no header exists
  • Image preprocessing: grayscale, 2x upscale, contrast sharpening & adaptive thresholding before OCR; below 5 readable characters we show an "Image Unreadable" prompt
  • Category detection first: any non-edible marker ("shampoo", "hair", "serum", "lotion", "wipe", "dermatologically tested", "external use only"...) forces a NON-EDIBLE verdict; food markers ("nutrition facts", "serving size", "ready-to-eat", "allergen advice"...) classify an edible product
  • Non-edible cosmetics get a strict "NON-EDIBLE COSMETIC/PERSONAL CARE PRODUCT" banner; edible foods get a green "EDIBLE PRODUCT" banner
Honesty rule

If OCR cannot read the label, we show "📷 Image Unreadable" and tell you how to retake the photo - we never guess ingredients.
