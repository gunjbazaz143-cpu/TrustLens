Privacy policy

1. What we collect

When you create an account we store your name, email and a hashed password. Scans store the content summary you submitted, the computed evidence and the result. Uploaded files are stored on our server with random names.

2. How it is used

Account data powers login, history, notifications and reports. Scan data powers your dashboard and is used only to produce and store verification results. We do not sell personal data.

3. Third-party analysis

Scanners may contact external services (WHOIS registries, news RSS feeds, or the target website you ask us to verify) to gather public evidence. Content you ask to be verified is sent to those services solely to perform the analysis.

4. Storage & retention

Data is stored in a local SQLite database. You can delete individual scans and reports at any time; contact us to delete your whole account.

5. Cookies & sessions

We use a session cookie for sign-in and a browser preference for the theme. No advertising or tracking cookies are used.

6. Security

Passwords are hashed, session cookies are HttpOnly, uploads use random filenames, and admin actions are audited.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy? Use the contact page.
