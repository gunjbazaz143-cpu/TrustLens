News scanner

Paste a headline, article text, a URL, or upload a screenshot / PDF of a story. The engine extracts the main claim, searches live trusted outlets, collects and compares evidence, then returns an evidence-backed verdict.

Click or drop a news screenshot or PDF
OCR extracts text from images · PDFs are parsed directly
How it works
  1. Claim is extracted from the headline, article, PDF or OCR'd screenshot
  2. People, places, organisations and dates are identified
  3. Targeted search queries are auto-generated from the claim
  4. Live search runs against trusted outlets only (RSS feeds + web search filtered to a trusted-domain allowlist)
  5. Matching articles are opened and compared - similarity, entity overlap, date consistency and contradiction
  6. Verdict + confidence are computed only from the evidence gathered
Trusted sources searched
Reuters · AP · BBC · The Guardian · Al Jazeera · The Hindu · Indian Express · Times of India · NDTV · PIB · WHO · UN News, plus any outlet on the trusted-domain allowlist (government sites, major wire agencies).
Verdicts
  • ✅ Verified - 2+ trusted sources support the claim
  • ⚠️ Partially Verified - some claims confirmed, some missing
  • ❌ Contradicted by Reliable Sources - trusted reports say the opposite
  • ❓ Insufficient Evidence - nothing found either way
Honesty rule

We never label a story "True" or "Fake" without evidence, and the AI (when enabled) only explains an already-computed verdict - it never decides it. If live internet search is unavailable, the report says so and returns Insufficient Evidence instead of guessing.
